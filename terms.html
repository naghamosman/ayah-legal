What Hemhema does

Hemhema is a Quran study and reference tool. It counts how often words, names, and themes appear across the Quran and gathers the verses for you (Explore); it matches a topic or feeling you type to verses on that theme (Ask); it helps you learn and recite the verses that matter to you (Learn, Your Verses, flashcards, streaks); it keeps a personal Diary of verses, links, and notes you save; it lets you send duaas and verses to friends; and, for leaders, it counts the topics in khutbahs you import and checks new material against your past khutbahs (My Khutbahs). It does not post, publish, or distribute anything on your behalf.

A note on religious content

Hemhema is a study aid, not a religious authority. Translations are provided for study and understanding; translation is interpretation, and counts based on the Arabic text may differ from counts in any translation. The app does not issue religious rulings (fatwas) and is not a substitute for learning with qualified teachers. If you use Hemhema to prepare talks or lessons, you are responsible for verifying the material.
