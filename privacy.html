Ayah

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Ayah ("we", "the app") is a Quran explorer: it counts how often a word, name, or theme appears across the Quran and gathers every verse in one place, with translation, transliteration, and audio, so the message sticks. The Quran text itself (all 6,236 verses) is bundled inside the app and read on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

What Ayah collects

  • Account information: your email address and sign-in credentials, managed through our authentication provider. You can sign in with Apple or with email.
  • Content you create or save: the verses you save, your Diary (saved verses, links, notes, and folders), your Your Verses recitation set and progress, your flashcards and streaks, the duaas you write, and, for leaders, the khutbah texts you import into My Khutbahs. This content is yours; it is stored so the app's features work for you. In the current build, Diary and duaa-sharing data is stored on your device only; when real accounts launch, it will sync to your account as described here.
  • Purchase information: if you subscribe, the transaction is handled by Apple In-App Purchase and our subscription provider (RevenueCat). We receive your subscription status (which tier, active or not). We never see or store your card number.
  • Usage analytics: anonymous, minimal product analytics about which features are used, so we can improve the app. No ad tracking, no cross-app tracking.

How your content is processed

Counting happens by code, not by AI. Explore counts words, names, and themes directly in the bundled Quran text. Ask maps the words you type to themes on your device, with no AI involved, and what you type in Ask is not sent anywhere for processing. Topics in your imported khutbahs are counted the same way.

Your content is not used to train AI models, not sold, and not shared with other users, except for the duaas and verses you choose to send to a friend through the app's sharing feature.

Friend sharing

When you send a duaa or a verse to a friend, the recipient sees the content you sent and your note. A shared verse is sent as a reference to the bundled Quran text, so the verse wording itself comes from the text on their device. Please only share content you are happy for the recipient to keep in their Diary.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data or your content.
  • We never use your content to train AI models.
  • We never post, publish, or share anything on your behalf.

Data retention and deletion

Your saved content stays in your account until you delete it. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings in the app; deletion removes your account, saved content, imported khutbahs, and duaas from our systems. Content stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Children

Ayah is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will note it here and in the app before the change takes effect.
